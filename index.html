About

Records, start to finish

I'm Louie Roz, an artist-producer based in Curaçao. I play bass and keys, build beats, record vocals and mix the records that come out of those sessions.

My sound sits between Dancehall rhythm, Hip-Hop energy and R&B harmony: dark, intimate and low-key, with room left around the vocal. I write and work in English and Papiamentu.

Every record I've worked on is on the credits page, with exactly what I did on it.
